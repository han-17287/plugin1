v0.31.0 authored architectural district routes

SCOPE AND LIMITS
Five distinct runtime layouts replace the simple trial corridor in
TraversalRhythmPractice: Production112,Living120,Administration114,Residence116,
Top118 tiles. Closed ceilings,ground routes,building maintenance paths,one central
hazard area,three grounded checkpoints/floor,and permanent recovery anchors.
Includes structural/facade scenery,existing pixel hero,compact HUD,vertical camera,
Main menu entry,and scene-loaded bootstrap for returning to Main.
Settings pause input/movement/mechanism clocks and clear buffered commands.
Existing Main patrol missions,weapons,bosses and saves were NOT migrated to these
coordinates. The district connection door is a map selector,not a story elevator.
Ancient-civilization gameplay remains second-run scope.

Unity6000.6.7f1 Editor is unavailable here. Native import,Play,physics,input,
URP rendering,visual quality,success rate,FPS and playthrough time were NOT measured.
The supplied Editor smoke fixtures were compiled with supplemental stubs,NOT run.
