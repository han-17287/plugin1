v0.30.0 five-floor traversal profiles and mechanism prototypes

SCOPE AND LIMITS
Added eight living/administration/residence/top Room Prefabs and five floor profiles.
Together with production:10 Room Prefabs x3 difficulty profiles =30 trial combinations.
Implemented mechanisms: timed searchlight exposure, periodic physical shutter with
safe-close deferral, movement-owner queued vent lift, timed anchor availability with
lease protection. Room reset restores all timers/availability/anchor state.
The new four floors have20 planned sections and48 adapted challenge designs;
all five floors have25 rhythm sections and60 designs. Most design rooms, rewards,
weapons and bosses are not implemented by these trial variants. Main.unity's existing
map/story/bosses were NOT replaced. Ancient-civilization gameplay remains second-run scope.
M1 remains a provisional code-derived envelope. Unity6000.6.7f1 Editor is unavailable:
native package import, Play, input, physics/lighting, success rate, retry timing,
FPS and full playthroughs were NOT run or measured.

NEW MECHANISM COVERAGE
FloorTraversalChecks sweeps floor/tier/anchor-motion phases, range reserve, complete
capsule roof clearance, forward framing, the five-tile shutter waiting ledge,
periodic timing and scan/shutter overlap. FloorMechanismChecks executes the shipping
TraversalFloorMechanism with narrow engine/player-owner doubles: harmless teaching
scan, sustained-exposure retry, pause, leaving beam, safe-close deferral, initial
opening reset, vent request only for ordinary motion, leased timed sockets, closed
availability and retry reset. It does NOT execute real body integration/occlusion.
The existing shipping grapple controller suite now verifies that clicking an inactive
socket cannot unexpectedly choose a wall behind it. Native PlayerStateMachine is not
simulated by that suite; its actual vent-force integration still needs Unity testing.
