The field renderer plays individual sampled parts through FluidSynth. The
sample bank and synthesizer are external dependencies and are not included
in the game's Ogg assets or the project archive. The gayageum, geomungo,
janggu and buk parts in this rendering are generated by the renderer.
The fifteen boss mixes use their own original multitrack recordings.
